On a phone: use the buttons either side of the picture, and hold the phone sideways. Controls on a computer. Left / Right = move the engine lever one notch (Left is toward the bow: ahead. Right is back: neutral, then astern). Down = let line out. Up or Space (or hold the mouse) = wind. F or a click = throw the dart at the boat. F11 = full screen.
  • Helm: the lever stays where you put it. The bow points into the current. In neutral you drift back with the current; click ahead and add throttle until your speed over ground (SOG) is near zero and the line hangs straight down. The current changes through the day, so keep bumping in and out of gear.
  • Drop: let the rig down in stages. Free-spool too long without a pause and the leader wraps the main line.
  • Find the zone: when the weight touches bottom the rod tip goes slack. Wind up 40 to 100 ft. The bottom changes as you move, so keep tending it. Leave the weight on the bottom and it hangs up.
  • The bite: watch the ROD TIP box. A swordfish bite is a small tap-tap, the tip getting heavy, or the tip going light. Wind hard to come tight. If he is not there, drop the bait back to him and wait for the next tap.
  • The fight: let him run when he sounds. Wind fast when he swims up at you. Do not wind while he jumps. Too much pressure pulls the hook from his soft mouth; slack lets it fall out.
